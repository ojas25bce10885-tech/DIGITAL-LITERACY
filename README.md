## Project Overview
This Digital Literacy Project equips students with essential skills to thrive safely in today's digital landscape. Serving as Student Digital Ambassador, I developed practical resources to guide peers toward responsible online behavior and professional digital communication.

The portfolio spans five core modules addressing digital awareness, personal branding, collaborative tools, professional etiquette, and cybersecurity. All deliverables are organized in this GitHub repository with detailed documentation and real-world outputs.

## Module Summary
🔹 **Module 1: Digital Literacy Awareness**  
Designed a compelling infographic using Canva that defines digital literacy and shares essential safety tips for students.

🔹 **Module 2: Student Digital Portfolio**  
Established professional accounts on GitHub, LinkedIn, and Kaggle to create a strong online presence for future opportunities.

🔹 **Module 3: Coding & Collaboration Platforms**  
Completed coding challenges on HackerRank and built an interactive Google Form quiz for peer engagement.

🔹 **Module 4: Email Etiquette & Communication**  
Crafted professional email templates and created a social media responsibility checklist for college students.

🔹 **Module 5: Cybercrime Awareness**  
Authored a detailed phishing case study with prevention strategies tailored for Indian college students.
